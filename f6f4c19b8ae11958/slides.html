How the agents work

Six agents hand structured data to each other

  • 4 of 6 agents are plain code: cheap, fast, tested.
  • 2 use gpt-6-luna: customer questions and fix wording.
  • Only verified findings reach the score and the fixes.

Safety

A hidden attack was ignored

  • Prompt injection: a page told the AI to give a perfect score. Ignored in 3 of 3 runs and reported as an issue.
  • No made-up findings: every AI claim needs a quote found on the page, or it is rejected.
  • Safe fetching: private addresses and redirects blocked, size, time and 3-page limits.
  • Spend caps: 5 analyses per visitor per day, 300 for everyone (about $0.28).
  • 60 automated tests pass.

Verifier note: “Detected 1 instruction-like text aimed at AI. Treated as data and reported, not obeyed.” The blocked page is reported too.

Honest limits and what is next

What does not work yet

Limits

  • The score is a checklist, not a validated prediction of AI citations.
  • JavaScript-built pages are not executed (Expargo showed 10 readable words).
  • Speed uses file sizes, not a real phone.
  • The AI step varies up to 7 points on subtle sites.
  • No customer has applied fixes yet, so no real before and after.

With another month

  • Real-browser rendering and real Core Web Vitals.
  • Validate the score against real AI answers.
  • Before and after with the three customers, then 10 or more.
  • Hosted version with accounts and history.
