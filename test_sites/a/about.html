About Lumen Desk Lamps

Lumen Desk Lamps was founded in 2021 in Portland, Oregon by lighting designer Maya Torres. Our goal is to make comfortable, flicker-free desk lighting affordable for everyone who works at a desk.

Our design approach

Every Lumen lamp is tested for flicker and color accuracy in our Portland studio before it ships.
