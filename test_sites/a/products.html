Lumen One and Lumen Pro: specs and prices

Lumen One, $59

Lumen One has 600 lumens of brightness, three color temperatures from 2700K to 5000K, and a 12 W power draw. It folds flat for storage and comes with a 2-year warranty.

Lumen Pro, $89

Lumen Pro has 900 lumens of brightness, five color temperatures from 2700K to 6500K, a USB-C charging port and a 15 W power draw. It comes with a 2-year warranty.

Shipping and returns

Shipping is free within the United States and takes 3-5 business days. You can return any lamp within 30 days for a full refund.
