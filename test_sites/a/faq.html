Frequently asked questions

How much do Lumen lamps cost?

The Lumen One costs $59 and the Lumen Pro costs $89.

Does shipping cost extra?

No. Shipping is free within the United States.

What warranty do the lamps have?

Both lamps include a 2-year warranty covering defects.

Can I replace the bulb?

The LED module is built in and rated for 50,000 hours, so there is no bulb to replace.
